He Meicen (1906–1982)

![image](/assets/imgs/hemeicen.jpg "He Meicen (first from right) with staff of the Chinese Dictionary Compilation Office"){: width="300" }

Owing to his family's scholarly tradition, He Meicen was broadly learned and rigorous in his research, with especially deep attainments in history. He was an invited contributor to "Wenshi (Shanghai)" and published articles in modern Chinese literary journals.

He served for many years as a compiler at the [Chinese Dictionary Compilation Office]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link glossary/compilation.md %}), participating in the compilation of the following dictionaries:

- "[National Language Dictionary]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link glossary/national-language-dict.md %})" (8 volumes), [Wang Yi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangyi.md %}) (editor-in-chief), compiled by Xu Yishi, [Sun Chongyi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/sunchongyi.md %}), [Wang Shuda]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangshuda.md %}), Peng Xinru, Xu Shirong, He Meicen, [Fu Yan]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/fuyan.md %}), and [Niu Wenqing]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/niuwenqing.md %}), Commercial Press (1945)
- "National Language Dictionary" (4 volumes), [Li Jinxi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/lijinxi.md %}) (editor-in-chief), [Wang Yi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangyi.md %}) (director), compiled by Xu Yishi, [Sun Chongyi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/sunchongyi.md %}), Xu Shirong, [Fu Yan]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/fuyan.md %}), [An Wenzhuo]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/anwenzhuo.md %}), [Wang Shuda]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangshuda.md %}), Zhang Weiyu, He Meicen, [Niu Jichang]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/niuwenqing.md %}), and Gao Jingcheng, Commercial Press (1948); reissued in 1962 under the new title "Chinese Dictionary"
- "National Pronunciation Dictionary with New Radical Index," [Li Jinxi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/lijinxi.md %}) (editor-in-chief), [Wang Yi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangyi.md %}) (director), edited and proofread by Xu Renyu, Fu Jieshi, [Niu Jichang]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/niuwenqing.md %}), Gao Jingcheng, He Meicen, Xu Shirong, [Sun Chongyi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/sunchongyi.md %}), [Wang Shuda]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangshuda.md %}), Zhang Naizhi, and Zhang Weiyu, Commercial Press (first edition, August 1949)
- "Annotated Zhonghua New Rhymes," [Li Jinxi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/lijinxi.md %}) (editor-in-chief), edited and proofread by [Wang Shuda]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangshuda.md %}), He Meicen, [Fu Yan]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/fuyan.md %}), [Sun Chongyi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/sunchongyi.md %}), Zhang Weiyu, and Zhang Naizhi, Commercial Press (first edition, July 1950)
- "Revised and Annotated Vocabulary of Common Characters in National Pronunciation," [Li Jinxi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/lijinxi.md %}) (editor-in-chief), edited and proofread by [Wang Yi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangyi.md %}), He Meicen, Fu Jieshi, [Niu Jichang]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/niuwenqing.md %}), Gao Jingcheng, Xu Renyu, Xu Shirong, [Sun Chongyi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/sunchongyi.md %}), [Wang Shuda]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangshuda.md %}), Zhang Naizhi, Zhang Weiyu, Li Zengdu, and Zhu Changqi, Commercial Press (first edition, August 1949)
- "Learning Culture Dictionary," [Li Jinxi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/lijinxi.md %}) (editor-in-chief), compiled by [Wang Shuda]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangshuda.md %}), He Meicen, Gao Jingcheng, [Sun Chongyi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/sunchongyi.md %}), Xu Renyu, Xu Shirong, and Fu Jieshi, Commercial Press (first edition, October 1952)
- "Homophone Dictionary," [Li Jinxi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/lijinxi.md %}) (editor-in-chief), compiled by He Meicen, [Sun Chongyi]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/sunchongyi.md %}), Xu Shirong, [Wang Shuda]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/wangshuda.md %}), Gao Jingcheng, and [Fu Yan]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link people/fuyan.md %}), Commercial Press (first edition, May 1956)

In July 1956, he became an editor in the Dictionary Editorial Office of the Institute of Linguistics at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, participating in the compilation of the "Modern Chinese Dictionary" (trial edition) as head of the Philosophy and Social Sciences group. During the Cultural Revolution he was sent down to the "May 7th Cadre School" in Henan for labor, but he persevered in his scholarly ideals despite the hardships. In August 1982, he died of acute nephritis at the age of 76. The academic community held him in very high regard; the Institute of Linguistics at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences offered an elegiac couplet — "Learned as five carts of books, a life of integrity rivaling the ancients; a household bare but for a few volumes of dictionaries handed down to posterity" — affirming his character and scholarship.
